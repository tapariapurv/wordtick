## Description
Wordtick turns words into time. Select some text or use your whole document, and Wordtick tells you how long it takes to read, so you can time a speech, script or essay before you deliver it.

- Two modes: Reading Aloud (100 / 130 / 160 wpm) and Silent Reading (150 / 250 / 400 wpm)
- Slow, Average and Fast presets, or type your own words-per-minute
- Use your selection, your whole document, or paste text or a word count
- Free, no account, no ads. Your text is only counted and never stored or sent anywhere.

Also available for Google Docs and on the web at wordtick.vercel.app.
