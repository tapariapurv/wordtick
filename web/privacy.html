Privacy, Terms & Support

Privacy

Wordtick does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. On the website, the text you paste is counted in your browser and never sent anywhere. In the Google Docs add-on and the Microsoft Word add-in, the text of your document or selection is read only to count words and show the result on your screen. It is not saved, logged or transmitted to us or to anyone else. We do not use cookies or analytics.

The Google Docs add-on uses the permission "view and manage the current document" only so it can read the document you have open, and "display and run third-party web content in prompts and sidebars" only to show the sidebar. It never edits your document.

Terms

Wordtick is free to use and provided as is, without warranty. Reading times are estimates based on average speeds (reading aloud 100/130/160 words per minute, silent reading 150/250/400).
