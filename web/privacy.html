Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Wordtick ("Wordtick", "we", "us") handles information when you use the Wordtick website at wordtick.vercel.app (the "Website"), the Wordtick add-on for Google Docs (the "Docs Add-on") and the Wordtick add-in for Microsoft Word (the "Word Add-in"), together the "Service". Wordtick is developed and operated by Purv Taparia, an independent developer. You can reach us at tapariapurv@gmail.com.

In short: Wordtick counts the words in text you give it and shows you how long that text takes to read or speak. All of this happens on your device. We do not collect, store, sell or share your personal data or the content of your documents.

1. Information we access

Website. Text you type or paste into the calculator is processed by JavaScript running in your browser. It is never sent to our servers or to anyone else.

Docs Add-on (Google user data). When you open the Wordtick sidebar and choose "Selection" or "Whole document", the add-on reads the plain text of your current selection or of the document you have open. This is the only Google user data Wordtick accesses. It does not access your name, email address, contacts, Google Drive files, other documents, comments, revision history or any other account information.

Word Add-in. In the same way, the Word Add-in reads the text of your current selection or open document through the Office JavaScript API.

2. How we use that information

Document text is used for one purpose only: to count its words and display the estimated reading and speaking time in the sidebar or task pane on your screen. The text is held in memory only while the count is calculated and is discarded as soon as the result is shown. Wordtick never edits, saves, copies, logs or uploads your document.

3. Google OAuth scopes

The Docs Add-on requests only these two permissions:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/documents.currentonly ("View and manage documents that this application has been installed in"). Used only to read the text of the document you have open, at the moment you ask for a count. Wordtick never modifies the document, even though Google groups read and write access into this one scope.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/script.container.ui ("Display and run third-party web content in prompts and sidebars inside Google applications"). Used only to show the Wordtick sidebar.

4. Limited Use disclosure

Wordtick's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

  • We use Google user data only to provide the word count and reading-time feature that is visible in the add-on's user interface.
  • We do not transfer Google user data to third parties.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising, including personalized, retargeted or interest-based advertising.
  • No human reads your data. We do not use it to develop, improve or train any artificial intelligence or machine learning models, or for any other purpose.
  • We do not sell Google user data or use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending.

5. Storage and retention

We do not store any document content or personal data. We have no database, no user accounts and no server-side logs of your text. Because nothing is kept, there is nothing to retain or delete.

6. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade or share your personal data or document content with anyone. We have no advertising partners, data brokers or analytics providers.

7. Cookies, analytics and tracking

Wordtick does not set cookies and does not use analytics, tracking pixels, fingerprinting or advertising technology.

8. Service providers

Like any website, the Service relies on a few providers to deliver its files. They receive the technical information that every web request includes (such as your IP address, browser type and the time of the request), but never the text you count:

  • Vercel Inc. hosts the Website and serves the script and stylesheet that the Docs Add-on and Word Add-in load. Vercel may keep standard server logs for security and operations under its own privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts serves the Rubik typeface. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Google runs the Docs Add-on inside Google Docs and Microsoft runs the Word Add-in inside Word. Your use of those products is covered by Google's and Microsoft's own privacy policies.

9. Security

All Wordtick files are served over HTTPS. Because your document text never leaves your device and nothing is stored, there is no stored data that could be breached.

10. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU and UK GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act give you rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to provide or delete, but you can contact us at any time with questions. You can stop all access by the Docs Add-on by uninstalling it, or by removing it at myaccount.google.com/permissions. You can remove the Word Add-in from Word's "My Add-ins" list.

11. Children

The Service is a general-audience writing tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

12. International users

The Service can be used from anywhere. Because processing happens on your own device, your document text is not transferred to any other country by Wordtick.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change how Wordtick handles information, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect. If a change would let us collect or use data in a new way, we will ask for your consent first where the law or Google's policies require it.

14. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: tapariapurv@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 5 business days.
