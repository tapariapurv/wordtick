wordtick

install in Microsoft Word

First download the add-in file, then load it into Word. Pick the version of Word you use.

Step 1: get the file

Download wordtick-manifest.xml

It's a small file that tells Word where Wordtick lives. Nothing else is installed on your computer.

Step 2: load it into Word

Word on the web (easiest, works on any computer)

  1. Open a document at office.com.
  2. Click Home → Add-ins → More Add-ins. In the dialog open My Add-ins.
  3. Click Upload My Add-in, choose wordtick-manifest.xml, and click Upload.

    Wordtick opens beside your page. Select some text and watch the time.

Word for Mac

  1. Quit Word. In Finder, press Shift+Cmd+G and go to ~/Library/Containers/com.microsoft.Word/Data/Documents.
  2. Create a folder named wef there if it doesn't exist, and put wordtick-manifest.xml inside it.
  3. Open Word and a document. Click Insert → Add-ins → My Add-ins, open the Developer Add-ins section, and choose Wordtick.

Word for Windows

  1. Easiest: use Word on the web above.
  2. Or in Word, click Insert → Add-ins → My Add-ins → Manage My Add-ins → Upload My Add-in and choose the file. This option isn't shown in every version.

Wordtick reads your selection only to count words. It never edits or sends your document anywhere. Sideloaded add-ins are marked as "developer" add-ins until Wordtick is listed in Microsoft's store.
